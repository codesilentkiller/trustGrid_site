Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing and plans

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime? +

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and you'll only be charged the prorated difference.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and wire transfers for Enterprise plans. Invoices are generated monthly or annually based on your billing cycle.

Is there a setup fee or hidden costs? +

No setup fees. No hidden costs. The price you see is exactly what you pay. Enterprise plans may require custom contracts based on volume and features.

Do you offer a free trial? +

Our Free tier is essentially a free trial. You get 100 requests/min with basic features. No credit card required. Upgrade anytime when you're ready to scale.

What happens if I exceed my rate limit? +

If you exceed your rate limit, additional requests will be rejected with a 429 status code. You can monitor your usage in your dashboard and upgrade your plan if needed.
