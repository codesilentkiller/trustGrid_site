Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with TrustGrid

How quickly can we integrate TrustGrid? +

Most customers integrate TrustGrid within 5 minutes. Our API is designed to be simple, with clear documentation and code examples in multiple languages.

Do you offer a free trial? +

Yes, we offer a free tier that includes 100 requests/minute. No credit card required. Perfect for testing and small-scale integration.

No setup fees. Our Pro plan is month-to-month with no long-term commitment. Enterprise plans may include annual contracts for volume discounts.

Free tier includes email support. Pro includes priority email support. Enterprise includes 24/7 phone support with dedicated account manager.

Enterprise customers can leverage custom ML models and tailored rule configurations based on their specific fraud patterns. Contact our sales team for details.
